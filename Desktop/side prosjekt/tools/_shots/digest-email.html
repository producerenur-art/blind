Brunch Electronik Returns to Barcelona with Record Growth
Brunch Electronik expands to three days (August 7-9, 2026) at Barcelona's Parc del Fòrum with a Sunday program for the first time, following record attendance of 65,000 in 2025. The lineup features major acts including Eric Prydz, Kaytranada, Jamie Jones, and Paul Kalkbrenner.

Trance Revival Dominates Festival Main Stages in 2026
Trance is experiencing a structural resurgence with headline DJ sets at major festivals like Tomorrowland, EDC, and Ultra. The revival is driven by returning legends, the trance/techno crossover, and Bryan Kearney's ASOT Tune of the Year win.
Source: TRANCE NEXUS
Hard Techno Explodes: Afro House Up 778% in 2026
The Splice and MIDiA Research Sounds of 2026 report reveals massive growth across harder subgenres: afro house up 778%, speed garage up 625%, and hard techno posting triple-digit growth. The BPM spectrum from 87 to 165 is active at every point within the same festival weekend.
