Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 July 2026

This privacy policy explains how SiriusFM ("we", "us", "the service") collects, uses and protects information about you when you use our website and app (web, Android, Windows and iOS). We comply with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

1. Who is responsible

SiriusFM is operated by a private party in Norway. Contact for privacy questions: post@siriusfm.no.

2. What information we collect

  • Account information: email address and password (encrypted) when you create a profile.
  • Profile content: display name, biography, banner image, theme and content you choose to share (posts, music, mixes).
  • Purchase information: when you buy in the marketplace, payment is handled by Stripe. We do not store your card number.
  • Technical information: necessary cookies and similar for login and operation.

We never sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising.

3. Why we process the information

  • To create and operate your user account.
  • To deliver the service: radio, social feed, chat, sharing of music.
  • To carry out purchases and sales in the marketplace.
  • To send necessary emails (e.g. confirmation and login).

4. Data processors we use

  • Supabase – login, database and profile storage.
  • Stripe – payments in the marketplace.
  • Resend – sending of email.
  • Gun.js – decentralized storage of parts of the social content.
  • Vercel – operation and hosting of the website.

These process information on our behalf under data processing agreements. Some may store data outside the EU/EEA; in that case lawful transfer mechanisms are used.

5. Retention period

We store the information for as long as you have an active account. If you delete your account, your personal data is removed, except for what we are legally required to keep (e.g. purchase history for accounting).

6. Your rights

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction and data portability, as well as to withdraw consent. Contact us at post@siriusfm.no to exercise your rights. You can also complain to the Norwegian Data Protection Authority.

7. Children

The service is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information about them.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. Significant changes will be announced on the website, and the date at the top is updated.

Do you have questions about privacy? Contact post@siriusfm.no.
